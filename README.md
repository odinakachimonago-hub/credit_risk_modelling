Credit Risk Modelling & Lending Decision Engine
AI-Powered Risk Assessment, Exposure Analysis & Explainable Lending Decisions
The Credit Risk Modelling & Lending Decision Engine is a full-stack risk application built to simulate real-world underwriting, portfolio monitoring, and risk-based lending decisions within a credit environment.
The platform demonstrates how analytical modelling, exposure analysis, machine learning, and technical execution can be combined to support practical and explainable credit risk decisions.
To get a practical feel for how I approach risk modelling, exposure analysis, and decision-support, users can run a simple simulation using borrower profiles and adjust key variables to see how changes in exposure, behaviour, and affordability impact lending outcomes. This project reflects my ability to combine commercial awareness, technical execution, and risk-first analytical thinking to solve exposure-based business problems.

Core Objectives
Simulate real-world credit risk decisioning
Assess borrower-level exposure and affordability

Probability of Default (PD) Modelling
A machine learning-based model estimates the Probability of Default (PD) using behavioural and credit-related variables.
Variables include:
Credit score
Previous defaults
Missed payments
Utilisation ratio
Existing debt
Monthly repayments
Payment behaviour
Arrears
Exposure Analysis
The system evaluates:
Exposure at Default (EAD)
Existing borrower exposure
Credit utilisation
Debt-to-income ratio
Affordability pressure
This supports stronger lending and exposure decisions.
Expected Loss Modelling
Risk calculations simulate:
Expected Loss = PD × LGD × EAD
Used to estimate potential portfolio and borrower-level loss exposure.
